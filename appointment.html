Before You Come

A little preparation
goes a long way.

You do not need to arrive with a finished plan. But a few things make the first conversation more useful.

  • A rough idea of the occasion and date
  • A photo of something you like, if you have one
  • An idea of your usual size and fit preferences
  • Any colours or fabrics you love — or cannot stand
  • Your questions, however small
Good to know

Appointments are one-to-one. Exact location details are shared when your fitting is confirmed.

More ways to reach us ↗
Is there a fee for the consultation?+

No. The first conversation is free — we want to understand what you need before either of us commits to anything.

Can I bring someone with me?+

Of course. Many clients bring a partner, friend or family member to help with fabric and design decisions.

How early should I book for a wedding?+

As early as you can. The earlier we start, the more time we have for cloth, fittings and any adjustments. Send us your date and we will advise.

What if I need to change my appointment?+

Just message us on WhatsApp. We understand that plans move and will find another time that works.
